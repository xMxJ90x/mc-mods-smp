Step 3: Where the files go

Open your game folder: press Win + R, type %appdata%\.minecraft and press Enter. On Mac it is ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft.

  • Mods (.jar files) go in the mods folder. Create it if it's missing. Don't unzip them or put them in sub-folders.
  • Resource packs (.zip files) go in the resourcepacks folder. Leave them zipped.

Step 4: Turn on the resource packs

  1. Launch Minecraft with the fabric-loader profile.
  2. Go to Options → Resource Packs and move all two packs to the right-hand list.
  3. Put Fresh Animations: Player Extension above Fresh Animations.

Troubleshooting

  • Crashes on launch: check you chose the Fabric profile, every file is for 26.2, and no mod is missing. Iris needs Sodium, and AmbientSounds needs CreativeCore.
  • Mobs look stretched or broken: make sure both EMF and ETF are in the mods folder, plus the two EMF Compat mods.
  • Low FPS: Fresh Animations is heavy. Lower animation distance in the Entity Model Features settings, or lower render distance.
  • Check it loaded: the Mods button on the title screen (Mod Menu) lists everything installed.
  • Voice chat doesn't connect: it needs the matching server plugin, so it only works on the server I send you.
These are client-side. They change what you see on your own screen, and other players see normal Minecraft unless they install them too.
